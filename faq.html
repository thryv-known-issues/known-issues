The page applies a threshold rule. P0 and P1 issues are always shown regardless of volume. P2 and P3 issues need at least 3 pending reports to appear. If only one or two clients have hit a P3, it's being handled case-by-case and doesn't need a dedicated row yet. If volume climbs to 3+ pending, it auto-promotes onto the page.
